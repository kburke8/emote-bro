🎮 How to Play

Controls

  • WASD or arrows — move
  • E — interact (buy EmoteBro, open shop, pull lever, steal, drop carried bro at base)
  • Space — swing mace (stun nearby player)
  • T — teleport to your base
  • G — spin the daily wheel (when ready)
  • L — toggle your base lock (while standing on lock slab)
  • Walk onto your money slab to collect coins.
  • Walk onto your lock slab and press E to lock your base.

How to win

There is no winning. Get rich. Collect EmoteBros. Steal from the others. Don't get robbed.

MVP notes (prototype timings)

  • Base lock: 30s (spec says 2 min — compressed for testing)
  • Events: every 60s (spec says 1 hour)
  • Daily spin: 2-min cooldown (spec says 24 hrs)
